# Microsoft Store listing (copy into Partner Center)

**Product name**: Activity Tracker

**Short description** (≤ 100 characters):
Screen time for Windows: see where your hours go, app by app. Private, light, built for 3D artists.

**Description**:

Activity Tracker shows how much time you really spend on your PC, and in which apps, without sending anything anywhere.

• Real screen time: only the time you actively use your PC counts. Breaks, sleep and a locked screen are left out.
• Your day, app by app: a timeline, minutes per hour and a daily goal ring.
• A Wrapped every week: your profile of the week (Marathoner, Night owl, Zen…), your top 5 apps, your records, shareable as one image.
• Time per project for 3D artists: Maya, Blender, Houdini, Photoshop, Nuke, After Effects… the scene or file you work on is detected automatically. All Maya windows count as Maya.
• Wellbeing: break reminders, a daily screen-time goal and per-app limits, as Windows notifications.
• Your year at a glance: one square per day, creative streaks and records.
• Make it yours: rename apps, change their logos, export everything to CSV.

Light by design: a ~2 MB app that takes a quick look every 2 seconds at low priority, then goes back to sleep. No keyboard or screen recording.

Private by design: your data never leaves your computer. No account, no telemetry, no ads.
